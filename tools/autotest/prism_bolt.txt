# Prism Bolt, Lv 1 to 10 (see the Prism Bolt spec), on husks that can't move (100 health each;
# their health after each cast goes to the log, in the order they were summoned).
# 1: Lv 1 into a husk with three behind it (the shards fan on into them).
# 2: Lv 4 down a line of four: the bolt bursts through the first and on into the second.
# 3: Prismatic Lance down the same line: all four pierced, no bursts.
# 4: Geode Burst into a husk ringed by seven (none in front of it): shards all round.
# 5: Lv 7: two bolts side by side (a shot in flight).
# 6: Lv 6 Crystal Shell: the caster's absorption after three hits (6).
# 7: Crystal Spire: a spire grows at the middle husk and picks off the ring.
# 8: Prism Barrage held for a second and a half on the line, at husks of 400 health (the HUD's
#    mana shows the cost).
