# JEI (an optional companion mod, loaded in the dev game): the mod's items in its list, each
# element's wands and staves on their own; the info pages for a Sovereign Heart and Fire Essence;
# the recipe for a Fire Archmage Staff.
hud on
cmd gamemode survival
cmd effect give @s minecraft:instant_health 1 10 true
cmd time set noon
wait 40
screen inventory
jei_filter @elementalarcana
wait 20
shot jei_items
jei_filter staff
wait 10
shot jei_staves
jei_show elementalarcana:sovereign_heart fire
wait 20
shot jei_heart_info
jei_show elementalarcana:fire_essence
wait 20
shot jei_essence_info
jei_show elementalarcana:archmage_staff fire
wait 20
shot jei_archmage_staff
jei_filter
screen close
wait 5
quit
