# Credits

All original assets in this mod were made for it and fall under the mod's MIT license:

- **Textures** (`textures/spell/*.png`, `textures/mob_effect/mana_sickness.png`): pixel art generated by `tools/gen_textures.py`.
- **Sounds**: `sounds/magic/awaken.ogg`, `sounds/magic/level_up.ogg` and `sounds/spell/fizzle.ogg` are synthesized with sox by `tools/gen_sounds.sh`. `sounds/spell/icicle/impact*.ogg` are synthesized with numpy/scipy by `tools/gen_spell_sounds.py`. No samples are used.
- **Particles** (`textures/particle/*.png`) and the icicle model texture (`textures/block/icicle.png`): generated by `tools/gen_textures.py`.

All other sounds and particles are Minecraft's own; the mod references them but does not redistribute them.

The mod contains no third-party assets. Any added later must be CC0 or public domain and listed here.
