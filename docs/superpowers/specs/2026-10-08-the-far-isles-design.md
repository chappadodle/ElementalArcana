# The Far Isles: the End's magic

## Why

The Overworld and the Nether have the mod's magic; the End has only levels. The story ends when the
Hollow is bound, and the End's outer islands (level 65 and up since the balance pass) are the
natural place for what comes after: the sky beyond the sky, where the first mages went to read the
stars. One compact arc: a place to find, a puzzle, a creature, its trophy, and Caelith's last word.

## Starfallen Observatories (outer End islands)

- The first mages' observatories, on the outer islands at least 1000 blocks from the centre (one
  in a while, kept clear of End cities): a round terrace of end stone bricks and purpur on the
  island's top, under a ring of four pillars, and in the middle an **Astral Orrery** (an amethyst
  and copper instrument on a dais) with a **sealed vault** below it.
- **The star lenses:** four **Star Lenses** stand on the pillars' feet, each showing one of four
  constellations (the Flame, the Wave, the Gale, the Stone: drawn on its face). Use a lens to turn
  it to the next.
- **The chart:** the orrery shows the night's chart, the four constellations in an order of their
  own (set when the observatory was made). Turn the lenses so that, going round from the north,
  they match it, and the orrery wakes: the vault opens.
- **The vault** holds the observatory's treasure: two Tomes of Insight, a Wishing Star, ender
  pearls, amethyst, and an **Astral Chart** (see below). Once open it stays open.
- A few **Stargazers** drift about every observatory.
- The Seeker's Compass finds the observatories in the End.

## The Stargazers

- Tall, thin robed figures of the outer islands, kin to no one: violet-black robes, a face of
  starlight, a halo of slowly turning motes. They drift slowly over the outer islands, and leave
  you be unless you strike them (or look them in the face, like an enderman).
- Angered, they blink about (a few blocks at a time) and cast **star shards**: three slow bolts of
  starlight that home a little and burst. Their touch draws you toward the void's edge: Levitation
  for a second.
- They leave **Stardust** (one or two) now and then, and very rarely an Astral Chart.

## The trophy: the Voidwalker's Charm

- Four Stardust round an ender pearl (and a chorus fruit beneath): carried (or in the Charm Pouch),
  if you fall into the void you're carried back to the last ground you stood on, with Slow Falling
  for a few seconds. Once every five minutes.

## The Astral Chart

- Used in the End: a beam of starlight points to the central island's exit portal for a minute
  (your way home from the far isles). Used anywhere else: it shows the nearest observatory's
  direction, as the compass does. It isn't used up.

## Caelith's last word

- An epilogue chapter after the Hollow is bound, **Beyond the Last Sky**: "Find where we read the
  stars." Its task: wake an orrery. Caelith's last words; the reward: a Wishing Star and XP.

## Also

- Advancements: **The Far Isles** (open an observatory's vault) and **Starstruck** (defeat a
  Stargazer).
