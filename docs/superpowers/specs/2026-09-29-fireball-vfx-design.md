# Fireball visual and sound effects: design

Date: 2026-09-29
Status: direction approved in conversation (concept sheet shown); built step by step, each step
play-tested

## Direction

- **Style:** pixel art plus glow. Textures and particles stay 16×16 Minecraft pixel art, animated,
  and light is real (additive glow halos, bright cores).
- **Tiers:** the fireball burns hotter as it levels, and each branch has its own look.

| Variant | When | Palette | Glow |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heat 1 | Lv 1–2 | deep red-orange | orange, soft |
| Heat 2 | Lv 3–4 | orange, yellow core | orange-gold |
| Heat 3 | Lv 6–7 (and Lv 5 without a look branch) | yellow-white core | gold |
| Heat 4 | Lv 8–9 | white-hot core, gold rim | pale gold, strong |
| Cluster | Cluster Bomb (Lv 5+), and its bomblets | dark shell with a few glowing seams | orange, dim |
| Meteor | Meteor (Lv 5+) | dark molten rock with lava cracks | orange-red |
| Sun | Sunfire, once fused | white-gold, a very bright core | white-gold, huge |
| Phoenix | Phoenix (Lv 10) | crimson to gold | red |

  **Priority:** Sun (once fused), then Phoenix, then Cluster or Meteor, then the heat stage. A
  Lv 10 Sunfire caster's fireballs show their Lv 5 branch look (or heat 4) until they fuse.
- **Sound:** mostly vanilla, layered and pitched per tier, plus a few custom signature sounds (Meteor
  impact, Sunfire, Phoenix). Each custom sound gets an A/B test with the user.

## Steps

1. **The fireball:** animated textures for all 8 variants and the glow halo.
2. **Particles:** a trail and embers that grow with the tiers, sparks gathering while it grows, and a
   throw burst.
3. **Explosions per tier:** shockwave rings and debris. Meteor gets a crater and a small camera
   shake, Sunfire a flash and corona, Cluster bomblets fuse sparks, and Phoenix wing-shaped trails.
4. **Sounds per tier:** layered vanilla, plus the signature custom sounds.

## Step 1 structure

- **The variant:** `SpellProjectile` gains a synced `variant` int (default 0).
- **`ProjectileSpell`:**
  - `model(variant)` defaults to `model()`.
  - `models()` lists every model to register.
  - `glow(variant)` defaults to null, meaning no halo.
- **`api/Glow`:** a record `(color 0xRRGGBB, size, intensity)`.
- **The renderer:**
  - It draws the variant's model.
  - Then, when there's a glow, it draws a camera-facing quad of `textures/misc/glow.png` (a soft
    radial white-to-black gradient) with `RenderType.eyes` (additive, full bright).
  - The quad is tinted `color × intensity`, sized `projectile size × glow size`, with a slight pulse.
- **Fireball:**
  - Picks the variant when conjured, and switches it to Sun when fused.
  - Bomblets are Cluster, and a mob's Meteor is Meteor.
- **Textures:**
  - `textures/block/fireball_<variant>.png`: 16 frames, each 16×16 and stacked. They're built from
    tileable noise that scrolls up one pixel a frame, so the loop is seamless, over a radial heat
    gradient (hot centre).
  - Each has `.mcmeta` animation data (frametime 2, interpolated).
  - Generated by `tools/gen_textures.py`.
- **Models:**
  - `models/spell/fireball.json` becomes the heat 1 model.
  - The other variants are one-line models, using the same model as parent with a different texture.

## Noted for later (from play-testing step 1)

- **Screen space with several held fireballs:** a full set, especially the Lv 8+ heat looks, can
  fill a large part of the screen. They could be made more subtle while held, for example smaller
  or with dimmer glow halos in first person. The user said to leave it for now.
