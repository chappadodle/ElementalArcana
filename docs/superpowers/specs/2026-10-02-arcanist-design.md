# People: the Arcanist

Date: 2026-10-02
Status: built (milestone 6 of `2026-10-02-fantasy-adventure-roadmap.md`).

Villages get a magic trader: the Arcanist, a villager profession with its own workstation. It buys
what mages gather and sells what they need, so emeralds and Essence become a second road to power:
gear, Essence of elements you never meet, Catalysts, and two items that only Arcanists sell.

## The Arcane Lectern

The Arcanist's workstation: a dark-wood lectern with gold fittings, an open book of glowing runes,
and a small violet crystal floating over it. It gives off a little light (7), and enchanting glyphs
drift toward the book, like an enchanting table's.

- Recipe (shapeless): a lectern, an amethyst shard and two Essence of any element.
- Any unemployed villager near one becomes an Arcanist, the way vanilla job sites work (point of
  interest `elementalarcana:arcanist`, in the `minecraft:acquirable_job_site` tag, without which
  villagers ignore a job site). Its work sound is the amethyst chime.

## The Arcanist

Purple robes with gold trim and a wide-brimmed hat (`textures/entity/villager/profession/arcanist.png`,
and a ragged version for zombie villagers). The usual five levels, two trades unlocked per level,
picked from these. Where a trade says "an element", each Arcanist picks one when the trade is
offered, so different Arcanists stock different things.

| Level | Buys | Sells |
|---|---|---|
| Novice | 2 Essence of an element → 1 emerald; 4 amethyst shards → 1 emerald | an Arcanist's Journal (1 emerald + a book); an Apprentice Wand of an element (4 emeralds) |
| Apprentice | 4 glowstone dust → 1 emerald | 1 Essence of an element (5 emeralds); an apprentice robe piece (6 emeralds) |
| Journeyman | | a Catalyst (Fire, Water, Wind or Earth; 20 emeralds + a diamond); an Adept Staff of an element (18 emeralds) |
| Expert | | an adept robe piece (16 emeralds); a Scroll of Unbinding (24 emeralds + 2 Essence) |
| Master | | a Tome of Insight (32 emeralds + a diamond); a Catalyst for less (12 emeralds + a diamond) |

## New items

- **Scroll of Unbinding** (uncommon, stacks to 16): read it to give back every skill tree node you
  bought, for free. Your element's free start nodes stay. Refunding nodes one at a time still costs
  Essence, so the scroll is the cheap way to rebuild.
- **Tome of Insight** (rare, one per stack): read it for one bonus tree point, like condensing
  Essence. It's consumed.

## In villages

Every village type (plains, desert, savanna, snowy, taiga) can build an Arcanist's cottage: a small
house of the village's own wood and stone with purple glass windows, an Arcane Lectern before a
wall of bookshelves, a bed, a chest (Essence, amethyst, books, lapis, sometimes a Scroll of
Unbinding) and someone living there. Nothing else in it is a job site, so that villager becomes
the Arcanist. It's added to each village's house pool with weight 1 (out of 70 to 90), like one of the
village's own small houses, so about one village in three has one (a village test showed weight 2
putting three in some villages). The cottage is a structure template generated by `tools/gen_people.py`,
and the pools are extended at server start (`ArcanistHouses`).

## Testing

- `tools/server_tests/arcanist.txt`: every village's cottage builds with its lectern, a villager by
  an Arcane Lectern takes the job, and (in the startup log) the cottage joins all five village pools.
- `tools/autotest/arcanist.txt`: the cottage outside and in, an Arcanist and its Master trades, the
  Tome and the Scroll being read, and six real villages searched for a cottage's lectern (the
  `find` step).
