# World I: elemental shrines

Date: 2026-10-02
Status: built (milestone 4 of `2026-10-02-fantasy-adventure-roadmap.md`).

Shrines are the first reason to travel: small open-air places of power where an element is strong.
They are a place to rest your mana, a daily blessing for mages, a way for those whose magic still
sleeps to seek an element, and a small offering chest.

## Where they are

One structure set (`worldgen/structure_set/shrines.json`): a random spread with spacing 20 and
separation 7 chunks, so one attempt per 20x20 chunk cell (about one shrine every 320 blocks where
the land suits one). Each cell picks one of the five kinds whose biomes match:

| Shrine | Biomes (`has_structure/<kind>_shrine`) |
|---|---|
| Fire | deserts, badlands, savannas |
| Water | beaches, rivers, swamps |
| Ice | snowy and icy biomes |
| Wind | mountains and windswept hills |
| Earth | plains and forests |

The shrine needs fairly level ground: the four corners and the middle of its 11x11 footprint may
differ by at most 6 blocks, and only Water shrines stand in water. The platform sits at the average
height, and the game's terrain blending (`beard_thin`) fills the ground below it and slopes the
ground above it away, so it settles into the land.

## What they look like

A round platform 11 blocks across in the element's stone, a ring of trim at its edge and an inner
ring around a pedestal. The Shrine Core floats on the pedestal: a crystal in the element's colour,
glowing (light 12) and shedding motes. Four pillars with caps stand around it (Earth has six
standing stones of uneven height instead, like a henge), and an offering chest sits in front.

| Shrine | Floor, trim, inner ring | Pillars and caps |
|---|---|---|
| Fire | polished blackstone bricks, polished blackstone, red nether bricks | blackstone, lit campfires |
| Water | prismarine bricks, dark prismarine, water | prismarine, sea lanterns |
| Ice | packed ice, blue ice, snow | packed ice, sea lanterns |
| Wind | calcite, smooth quartz, polished diorite | calcite, end rods |
| Earth | moss, mossy cobblestone, rooted dirt | mossy stone bricks, chiseled stone bricks |

The Shrine Core can't be broken or mined: the power stays where it was found.

## What they do

- **Place of Power:** within 12 blocks of a core, mana regenerates 1.5 times as fast, or twice as
  fast for a mage who holds the shrine's element family.
- **Discovery:** the first time a player uses a shrine, they get about three kills' worth of XP at
  their level.
- **Once a Minecraft day per shrine and player:**
  - A player whose magic still sleeps communes with it. There's a 1 in 2 chance that their magic
    wakes, leaning to the shrine's element: the usual awakening weights, so an Ice shrine may give
    Water, as kin. Otherwise the shrine stirs, and they can try again tomorrow.
  - An awakened player is blessed. Their mana is refilled, and for 10 minutes they get +5 Affinity
    for the shrine's element family and +2 Potency. The bonus is added with gear stats, so it
    shows in the Stats window.
- **The offering chest:** 2 to 4 rolls of the element's Essence, gold nuggets, emeralds, amethyst
  and bottles of enchanting. There's also about a 2 in 5 chance of one rarer find: an Apprentice
  Wand of the element, a Catalyst of the element's family, an apprentice hood or boots, or an
  Arcanist's Journal.

The Journal gains a page about shrines.

## Testing

- `tools/server_tests/shrines.txt`: each kind builds, and natural generation finds all five.
- `tools/autotest/shrines.txt`: the hidden client finds each kind far from the explored land (so
  it's generated by the current code) and screenshots it.
