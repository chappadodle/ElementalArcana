# Arcane Flora growing wild (see its spec): from far out in new land, the nearest of a biome each
# herb grows in (the biome step), then the nearest of that herb there (the find step logs up to five
# and views the first from 8 blocks south and 5 up): a shot of each. Deepcaps grow on cave floors, so
# they're only looked for (logged), not shot. In spectator mode, so the camera doesn't fall.
