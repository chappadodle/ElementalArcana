# Chain Lightning, Lv 1 to 10 (see the Chain Lightning spec), on husks that can't move (100 health
# each; their health after each cast goes to the log, in the order they were summoned).
# 1: Lv 1 down a row of six: four struck (5, 4, 3.2, 2.6). 2: Lv 4: five struck (5, 4.5, 4.05,
# 3.65, 3.28). 3: Storm Fork into a block of nine: the chain forks through all of them. 4: Overload
# into the same block: three struck, and bursts hurt their neighbours. 5: Lv 7: a bolt from the sky
# on the first. 6: Lv 8: the struck stay charged and arc to each other (a shot a second later,
# health before and after). 7: Ball Lightning loosed down an empty lane with husks to either side.
# 8: Thunder Lord held for a second and a half on the row (the HUD's mana shows the pulses' cost).
