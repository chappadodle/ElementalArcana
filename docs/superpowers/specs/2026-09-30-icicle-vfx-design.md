# Icicle visual and sound effects: design

Date: 2026-09-30
Status: direction approved in conversation. It's built in the Fireball's four steps
(docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-fireball-vfx-design.md) and reuses its systems:
- look variants with glow halos
- glowing particles
- marks painted onto blocks
- 3D meshes
- loops that follow the projectile
- signature sounds with an A/B switch
- dynamic lights

## Direction

**Cold deepens as it levels, and each branch has its own look.** Pixel art plus glow, as with the
Fireball. Every look gets an animated texture with a glint sweeping along it, and a pale, cold glow
halo.

| Look | When | Style | Model |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frost 1 | Lv 1–2 (and mobs) | pale, cloudy ice, barely glowing | icicle |
| Frost 2 | Lv 3–4 | clear icy blue | icicle |
| Frost 3 | Lv 6–7 (and Lv 5 with no branch yet) | deep glacial blue | icicle |
| Frost 4 | Lv 8–9 | crystal clear with a glowing cold core | icicle |
| Piercing Cold | Lv 5+ Piercing Cold | a long, slender, bright needle | needle |
| Shatterburst | Lv 5+ Shatterburst | cracked crystal with pulsing, glowing fracture lines | crystal |
| Endless Winter | Lv 10 Endless Winter | dark storm ice flecked with snow | icicle |
| Glacial Lance | once fused | a huge glacier-blue lance with a bright core | lance |

**Priority:** Lance (once fused), then Endless Winter, then the Lv 5 branch, then the frost stage.
A Glacial Lance caster's icicles show their Lv 5 branch look (or Frost 4) until they fuse.

## Steps

1. **Looks:** the 8 animated textures, the needle, crystal and lance models, and the glow halos.
2. **Particles:**
   - While held: frost gathering in, and cold vapor sinking.
   - Fully grown: a ring of glints.
   - Throw: a burst of crystal sparks.
   - In flight: a frost-mist trail with sparkles. Piercing streaks, Shatterburst crackles, and
     Winter trails snow.
3. **Impacts:**
   - 3D ice shards that bounce and glint.
   - A frost ring on the ground.
   - Frost marks painted onto the blocks, melting away from the edges.
   - Deep Freeze: a 3D ice shell around the target while it's Frozen, which shatters when the
     freeze ends.
   - Glacial Lance: 3D ice spikes erupting from the ground around the hit.
   - Endless Winter: a wide frost patch and a snow flurry.
4. **Sounds:**
   - Layered vanilla sounds (amethyst, glass, powder snow), and the synthesized shatter the user
     kept.
   - Loops: a crystalline hum while held, and a whistle in flight with the fly-by pitch shift.
   - ★ signature sounds, each A/B tested:
     - Glacial Lance: forging, launch, and a shattering quake
     - Endless Winter: a howling blizzard

**Dynamic lights:** icicles light up the dark as they fly, like fireballs but a little dimmer:
- Frost 1 gives 6 and Frost 2 gives 7. Frost 3, Frost 4, Piercing and Shatterburst give 8,
  Winter 9, and Lance 12.
- Lv 8+ adds 2.
- Held icicles brighten as they grow.
- Shatterburst shrapnel gives 4.

## Step 1 structure

- **The look:**
  - `IcicleSpell` gets look constants 0–7, in the table's order.
  - `lookFor(level, branch5, branch10)` picks the look, following the priority above.
  - Conjuring sets it; `fuse` sets Lance.
  - Mob icicles keep look 0 (Frost 1).
- **Models and glows:**
  - `model(variant)`, `models()` and `glow(variant)` work as they do for the Fireball.
  - `models/spell/icicle.json` becomes Frost 1. Frost 2–4 and Winter are one-line child models
    that change only the texture.
  - The new models are `icicle_needle`, `icicle_crystal` and `icicle_lance`, each with its own
    texture.
- **Textures:**
  - `textures/block/icicle_<look>.png`: 16 frames, each 16×16, animated (frametime 2,
    interpolated).
  - Each is a colour ramp over tileable noise, with a bright diagonal glint band crossing once per
    loop.
  - Style extras:
    - Frost 4 and Lance: a bright core stripe.
    - Piercing: streaks running along its length.
    - Shatterburst: crack lines whose glow pulses.
    - Winter: dark ice with snow specks drifting down.
  - All generated by `tools/gen_textures.py`.

## Step 2 structure (particles)

- **`IcicleEffects`** mirrors `FireballEffects`: all the icicle's client particles, keyed by look.
  It uses the glowing Flare and Spark particles in cold palettes, the frost sparkle and frost
  mist, and vanilla snowflakes.
  - The colder the look, the longer and brighter the trail and the less it mists.
  - Lv 8+ trails are 20% bigger and longer, with more sparkles.
- **Held:**
  - Anchored sparks gathering in.
  - Cold vapour (frost mist) sinking off it.
  - The odd glint once it's fully formed.
  - Winter drops snowflakes.
  - With more than 2 held, everything is halved, to keep a Halo from clouding the view.
- **Fully grown:** a flash and a ring of glints. The server keeps only the chime.
- **Throw:** a cold flash and a ring of crystal sparks around the throw's line (18 for the Lance).
- **Flight:**
  - A ribbon of frost flares along the path, with no gaps, plus sparkles and mist by palette.
  - Piercing leaves bright streaks along its line, and Shatterburst snaps off crackling sparks.
  - Winter trails snow, and Frost 1 sheds the odd snowflake.
  - The Lance has two glints spiralling around its path.
  - Shrapnel only glints.
