# Arcane Flora (see its spec). A garden in the air far out: the eight herbs in a row on their own
# grounds (sand on stone, grass, stone, snow, calcite, grass, grass, and a pool for the Moonlily),
# and the seven that pot, potted, behind them. The last run's garden is cleared before the ground is
# laid again (a herb whose ground is replaced pops off as an item, as a flower does), and none may
# lie about ("stray items" would be said).
# 1. By day (a shot): the Sunpetal open, the Moonlily shut.
# 2. At night (a shot): random ticks sped up for a few seconds so they turn: the Sunpetal shut, the
#    Moonlily open and glowing; the glowing herbs lit. Then close up (from spectator mode, so the
#    camera doesn't fall): the Moonlily, and the herbs with their motes.
# 3. Kinship's power: a Smite on a fresh level-40 husk, then another after a Tonic of Dawn's Radiance
#    Kinship (the damage logged: a fifth more).
