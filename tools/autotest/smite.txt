# Smite, Lv 1 to 10 (see the Smite spec), on creatures that can't move (100 health each; health
# after each cast goes to the log, in the order they were summoned). Husks are undead, spiders not.
# 1: Lv 1 on a husk, a spider beside it and a husk 3.5 blocks off: the far husk is spared (shots of
#    the mark and the pillar). 2: Lv 2 (Wide Judgment): the far husk is struck too.
# 3: Lv 4 (Consecration) at the caster's own feet, the caster hurt to half: they heal in it.
# 4: Sunlance: the beam follows the caster's aim from one husk to the next along a row.
# 5: Triple Judgment down a row of three husks 4 blocks apart (one pillar each).
# 6: Lv 9 (Radiant Burst): slowed husks (not NoAI: those ignore knockback) around the mark thrown
#    back (positions before, and just after it lands).
# 7: Wrath of Heaven on a ring of husks: small pillars rain round the mark.
# 8: Avatar of Light among husks: small pillars fall on them for 8 seconds.
