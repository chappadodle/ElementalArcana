# Wind Blade visual and sound effects: design

Date: 2026-09-30
Status: direction approved in conversation. It's built in the same four steps as the Fireball and
Icicle passes, and reuses their systems.

## Direction

**A slash of moving air, not a solid object.** The old blade was a flat, blocky arc of 11 boxes,
and it turned like a wheel while held. That turn was the renderer's generic slow spin for held
projectiles, which suits a ball or an icicle but not a flat crescent.

The new blade is drawn by its own renderer as a smooth, curved crescent ribbon:
- A sharp, bright cutting edge fading to clear behind it, tapering to fine points at both tips.
- Wind streaks flow along it (a scrolling texture), and it ripples slightly.
- **No spinning.** Held, it stays still with its edge facing out, and each blade in a fanned set
  keeps a fixed angle (a Cross Cut reads as an X). In flight it flies edge-first.
- **The one exception is Boomerang**, which turns in flight because boomerangs do.

**The air gets sharper, cleaner and brighter as the level rises, and each branch has its own
look.**

| Look | When | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Gust | Lv 1–2 (and mobs) | a soft, pale white-green wisp |
| Breeze | Lv 3–4 | a mint-green slash with a clearer edge |
| Gale | Lv 6–7 (and Lv 5 with no branch yet) | a bright jade slash with streaks racing along it |
| Tempest | Lv 8–9 | a brilliant white edge with a green glow (bloom) |
| Boomerang | Lv 5+ Boomerang | a doubled crescent, two arcs back to back, turning in flight |
| Tempest Edge | Lv 5+ Tempest Edge | a storm-grey and teal slash with dark swirling streaks |
| Thousand Cuts | Lv 10 Thousand Cuts (and its split blades) | three thin, razor-sharp parallel slashes |
| Storm Scythe | once fused | a huge, dark storm crescent with lightning crackling along its edge (bloom) |

**Priority:** Scythe (once fused), then Thousand Cuts, then the Lv 5 branch, then the tier. Lv 8+
brightens every look.

**Dynamic lights:** air doesn't glow, so only the Lv 8+ edge (a faint 4) and the Storm Scythe's
lightning (9) give light.

## Steps

1. **Looks:** the slash renderer, the textures, the 8 looks and the fixed fan angles.
2. **Particles:**
   - While forming: air drawn in.
   - Throw: a gust ring.
   - In flight: a wake of wind streaks, with leaves or dust kicked up near the ground.
   - Branch signatures.
3. **Impacts:**
   - A burst of air rings.
   - Cut marks painted onto the blocks it hits, fading.
   - Bits blown off the block.
   - Tempest Edge: its whirlwind becomes a 3D spinning funnel.
   - Storm Scythe: a lightning flash and a long cut across the ground.
4. **Sounds:**
   - Vanilla breeze and wind-charge sounds, and loops that follow the blade.
   - ★ signature sounds for Storm Scythe and Thousand Cuts, with the A/B switch.

## Step 1 structure

- **Custom visuals:** `client/visual/ProjectileVisuals` maps a spell to a client renderer that
  draws its projectile instead of a block model. `SpellProjectileRenderer` asks it first.
  `WindSlashRenderer` is registered for the Wind Blade.
- **The look:**
  - `WindBladeSpell` gets look constants 0–7, in the table's order, with a `BRIGHT` bit added
    at Lv 8+, as for the Icicle.
  - Conjuring sets the look. `fuse` sets Scythe, and split blades are Thousand Cuts.
  - Mob blades are Gust.
- **`WindSlashRenderer`:**
  - **Orientation:** held, it faces the caster's view; in flight, its motion. It's then rolled by
    a fixed fan angle for its place in the formation. Boomerang also turns around its own plane
    in flight.
  - **The ribbon:** a 140° arc lying flat, 20 segments, with its arch (convex side) leading the
    way. Its width
    tapers to 0 at the tips. The texture runs along the arc (U) and across it (V: edge bright,
    back clear). U scrolls with time, so the wind flows along it, and the ribbon ripples slightly.
  - **Layers:** the ribbon, then a wider, fainter copy as a soft glow. Both are additive and drawn
    from both sides.
  - **Per look:**
    - Thousand Cuts draws three thin ribbons stacked behind each other.
    - Boomerang draws its ribbon twice, the second turned 180° around the plane's normal.
    - The Scythe adds a jagged lightning line along its edge, redrawn every tick.
  - **Bloom:** Lv 8+ looks and the Scythe also draw into the bloom buffer (optional Veil bloom).
- **Textures** (`textures/misc/wind_slash_<style>.png`), grey (the look tints them), and tiling
  along U:
  - `soft`: Gust and Breeze.
  - `sharp`: Gale, Tempest and Boomerang.
  - `storm`: Tempest Edge and the Scythe.
  - `lines`: Thousand Cuts.
  - Generated by `tools/gen_textures.py`.

## Step 2 structure (particles)

- **`WindBladeEffects`**, like the Icicle's effects:
  - Wind curls (the tinted `SWIRL` particle) in each look's colour.
  - Vanilla small gusts and electric sparks.
  - Dust from the block it skims.
  - Glowing sparks for glints.
- **Held:** curls circling in toward the blade as it forms, fewer for sets of 3 or more. The
  Scythe crackles with electric sparks.
- **Fully grown:** a ring of wind snapping outward, and an anchored glint. The server keeps only
  its sound.
- **Throw:** a small gust at the hand and a ring of curls around the throw's line (16 for the
  Scythe).
- **Flight:**
  - Wind peels off both tips. The tips are placed like the renderer's, including its fan tilt
    (`WindSlashRenderer#fanAngle`, now shared).
  - It kicks up dust from the block it skims, within about 2 blocks of the ground.
- **Branch signatures:**
  - Boomerang: a spiral wake.
  - Tempest Edge: dark swirls.
  - Thousand Cuts: sharp glints along the edge.
  - The Scythe: electric sparks and bright bolts.
  - Lv 8+ tiers: the odd glint.
- Split Thousand Cuts blades are lighter.

## Step 3 structure (impacts)

- **One packet per impact:** `WindBladeSpell#impact` sends one `WIND_CUT` particle,
  `WindCutOptions(look, size, charge, flight direction, fan tilt, whirlwind)`, at the true impact
  point (`impactPoint` for creatures). It goes to players within 32 blocks, 48 for the Scythe.
  `WindCutEmitter` plays it out over 6 ticks through `WindBladeEffects#impact`. The impact sound
  stays on the server until step 4.
  - `WindBladeSpell#fanAngle` is now the one copy of the fan tilt, used by the renderer, the
    particles and the cut marks.
- **The impact:**
  - A small gust (a full gust for the Scythe), a flat air ring on the ground or a halo in
    mid-air, and a burst of curls.
  - A **cut mark** (`Decals.Kind.CUT`): three pale slashes on the struck surface. It's turned to
    run along the blade's edge, from the flight direction and fan tilt projected onto the surface,
    and fades over 5 s (8 s and 2.6 blocks for the Scythe).
    - Marks can now be turned. A turned mark is mapped at 1/√2 of its size, so its art has to sit
      inside the texture's inner circle, or the corners would repeat.
  - **Bits blown off:** debris cubes of the struck block, flung off its face and along the flight.
- **Tempest Edge:**
  - `WindFunnelParticle`: a 3D funnel for the whirlwind's 20 ticks. It's narrow at the ground and
    widens as it rises (2.4 blocks), twisting and turning, and throws off curls. The texture is
    `block/wind_funnel`, and it's drawn additively from both sides.
  - `WindVortex` keeps the pull and no longer draws streaks.
- **Storm Scythe:** a lightning flash with electric sparks and bolts.

## Step 4 structure (sounds)

- **`WindBladeSounds`** is like `IcicleSounds`: it plays on each client from the particle hooks,
  and the impact sound plays in `WindCutEmitter`.
  - The server no longer plays the conjure, throw, merge, fully-grown or impact sounds, or the
    mobs' blade shot. `WindVortex` keeps its whirl.
  - The Storm Scythe merge is heard when a held blade's look changes to Scythe.
- **Vanilla mixes, by look** (breeze and wind-charge sounds):
  - A soft wind while held, and a rushing wind in flight that follows the blade (fly-by pitch).
    The pitch runs from high and light for the low tiers to low and heavy for the Scythe, and
    Thousand Cuts gets a thin, high whistle.
  - Boomerang adds a whirring as it turns.
  - Under every impact: the wind-charge burst, sized by the blade. The keener looks add a sweep,
    and the Scythe adds lightning.
- **★ Signature sounds** (the `signatureSounds` switch):
  - Storm Scythe: `scythe_roar`, a dark storm-wind loop in flight with static crackles, and
    `scythe_strike`, a thunder crack, a wind blast and a rolling tail.
  - Thousand Cuts: `thousand_slash`, three razor swishes, on the throw and (higher) on impact.
